How do I book?

Click Book Now to choose your service and time online. Appointments can be booked up to a year ahead and at least 12 hours in advance.

What should I do before a wig install or quickweave?

Please come with your hair already braided down.

Do I need to bring hair?

For a Weave Ponytail, please bring 2–3 packs of hair. Tape-Ins can be booked with extensions included ($500) or with your own extensions ($245).

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Cash App are accepted.
